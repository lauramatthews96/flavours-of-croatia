You Are the Captain of Your Own Ship

At Flavours of Croatia Sailing Holidays, we believe that your holiday should be as unique as you are. That’s why we offer the option to design your very own itinerary. Whether you’re dreaming of private wine tastings, secluded bays, cultural landmarks, or gourmet dining, we’ll work with you to create a bespoke experience tailored to your preferences.

Your journey, your way

Imagine sailing along the stunning Croatian coastline, stopping exactly where your heart desires. Want to linger at a vineyard overlooking the Adriatic? Prefer more time exploring hidden coves or vibrant towns? We’ll help you craft a personalised itinerary that brings your vision to life.

Designed around you

What You Can Customise

When designing your itinerary, you can shape every hour of the week.

  • Choose destinations Select specific islands, towns, or landmarks to visit.
  • Tailor activities Add more time for wine tastings, swimming, snorkelling, or cultural tours.
  • Pick culinary experiences Request private dining, traditional meals, or gourmet pairings with local wines.
  • Set the pace Opt for a relaxed journey or a more adventure-filled schedule.
  • Add special requests Whether it’s celebrating a special occasion or creating themed stops, we’ll make it happen.
