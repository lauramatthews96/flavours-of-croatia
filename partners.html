Our Partners

Our Partners

At Flavours of Croatia Sailing Holidays, we proudly collaborate with some of Croatia’s most exceptional wineries. These esteemed partners represent the pinnacle of Croatian viticulture, offering unique blends of tradition, innovation, and unmatched quality. Each winery we work with provides not only remarkable wines but also unforgettable experiences for those who visit.

Kaštel Šikuli Winery

sikuli.hr @kastel.sikuli

Nestled in the picturesque region of Kaštel Novi, Kaštel Šikuli Winery is a family-owned gem blending generations of experience with modern innovation. Established in 2019, this boutique winery cultivates 13,000 square meters of lush vineyards, complemented by smaller family plots inland. Run by the passionate Kovačević family, their wines embody dedication, expertise, and a deep connection to the land. Visitors are invited to experience their intimate tastings paired with panoramic views of Split and the Dalmatian coast.

Prgin Winery

@prginwinery

Located above the breathtaking vineyards of Primošten, Prgin Winery epitomizes boutique winemaking at its finest. Focusing on small-batch production, Prgin crafts high-quality wines from indigenous Croatian grape varieties, ensuring each sip reflects the essence of the region. Their commitment to preserving tradition while embracing artisanal precision makes them a favourite for wine enthusiasts. Tastings at Prgin Winery offer a rare chance to savour exquisite wines in a setting of unmatched beauty and tranquillity.

Ante Sladić Winery

antesladicvino.hr @kastel.sikuli

Perched in the charming village of Plastovo, near Skradin and Krka National Park, Ante Sladić Winery is a family-run haven of authenticity and heritage. Specializing in wines made from indigenous Dalmatian grapes, they craft each bottle with respect for tradition and a focus on sustainable practices. The unique microclimate, influenced by the Krka River and the Adriatic Sea, lends their wines a distinct character. Visitors can enjoy an immersive tasting experience paired with local dishes and warm family hospitality.

Stina Winery

stina-vino.hr @stina_vino

On the island of Brač, Stina Winery turns the rocky terroir into liquid poetry. Known for its bold and contemporary approach, Stina honours its name, which means “stone” in Croatian, reflecting the challenging yet rewarding environment in which their vines grow. With a focus on modern design and top-quality winemaking, their labels are blank canvases, inviting you to create your own story. A visit to Stina Winery is a journey into the heart of Brač’s artistry, culture, and innovative spirit.

Testament Winery

testament-winery.com @testamentwinery

Located near Šibenik, Testament Winery offers a perfect blend of tradition and sustainable innovation. Perched on limestone hills overlooking the Adriatic Sea, their vineyards are a testament to Croatia’s rich winemaking history. Testament specialises in indigenous grape varieties, producing organic wines that tell a story of the region’s unique terroir. Their welcoming tasting sessions, paired with breathtaking views, provide an unforgettable introduction to Croatia’s coastal viticulture.

NCP charter

ncp-charter.com @ncp_charter

NCP Charter is one of Croatia’s most esteemed charter companies, offering an exceptional fleet of yachts and a reputation built on excellence and professionalism. Based in the heart of Dalmatia, their services combine top-quality boats with a deep understanding of the Adriatic’s unique charm. Whether you’re looking for a bareboat charter or a crewed sailing experience, NCP ensures seamless service and unforgettable adventures. Their commitment to providing a premium sailing experience aligns perfectly with our dedication to offering the finest sailing holidays in Croatia.
