Seven days · Šibenik to Split

Gourmet Coastal Experience

Gourmet Coastal Experience

Itinerary

Embark on a one-of-a-kind Croatian Sailing & Wine Adventure, a seven-day journey blending the Adriatic’s natural beauty, cultural richness, and world-class wines. Starting in the historic coastal town of Sibenik, this luxurious itinerary takes you through charming destinations such as Split, Primošten, and Skradin. Enjoy leisurely sailing days, swim in crystal-clear waters, and explore UNESCO-listed landmarks like Diocletian’s Palace and the Cathedral of St. James. Along the way, savor exclusive wine tastings at boutique, family-run wineries showcasing Croatia’s unique grape varieties, paired with authentic Dalmatian cuisine. This extraordinary experience offers the perfect balance of relaxation, exploration, and indulgence, creating lasting memories of Croatia’s dazzling coastline and vibrant culture.

Tours availble May - October 2024

* The tour is for a minimum of 4 to a maximum of 8 people people on a double occupancy per cabin

Day 1 - Saturday

Welcome to Sibenik

Your adventure begins at Marina Mandalina in Sibenik, where we’ll welcome you aboard our beautiful yacht in the afternoon. After settling in, enjoy a refreshing welcome drink as you get acquainted with your home for the week. The evening is yours to explore Sibenik, a charming coastal town known for its medieval architecture, including the UNESCO-listed Cathedral of St. James. Stroll along the waterfront promenade, enjoy dinner at a local konoba (tavern), or simply relax onboard under the stars.

Day 2 - Sunday

Sailing South

We cast off in the morning and set sail for Split, Croatia’s bustling cultural and historical hub. Feel the wind in your hair as we unfurl the sails and glide across the azure waters of the Adriatic. After a leisurely day at sea, we’ll drop anchor or moor in the harbour, where you can soak in the relaxed atmosphere. The evening features a special tasting of wines from our partner wineries, offering a perfect introduction to Croatia’s celebrated winemaking traditions.

Day 3 - Monday

Primošten’s Beauty and Boutique Wines

After a relaxed morning with breakfast and a refreshing swim, we set sail for the stunning town of Primošten. Famous for its sprawling vineyards and picturesque beaches, Primošten is home to Mala Raduča, often voted one of Croatia’s most beautiful beaches.

In the afternoon, we’ll visit Prgin Winery, perched above the region’s finest vineyards. This boutique winery focuses on small-batch, high-quality wines crafted from unique local grape varieties. After savouring their creations, we head downtown for an authentic Dalmatian dinner at one of our hand-selected restaurants. Expect fresh seafood, hearty stews, and flavours that showcase the region’s rich culinary heritage.

Day 4 - Tuesday

Kastela’s Rich Wine Heritage

Today’s journey takes us to Split/Kastela, with plenty of opportunities for swimming and a relaxed onboard lunch en route. Upon arrival, we’ll visit Kaštel Šikuli Winery, a family-run estate founded in 2019. This winery benefits from the Kovacevic family’s decades of expertise in winemaking.

During our visit, you’ll meet the passionate family behind the wines and sample their exquisite creations. The day culminates with a food and wine pairing dinner overlooking breathtaking views of Split, where each dish and wine is carefully selected to complement the other – a true feast for the senses.

Day 5 - Wednesday

Free Day in Split

Today is yours to explore Split, one of Croatia’s most vibrant cities. Wander through the ancient streets of Diocletian’s Palace, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, or soak up the energy along the Riva promenade. If you prefer a slower pace, unwind on one of Split’s stunning beaches, such as Bačvice or Kasjuni. The city offers an enticing blend of history, culture, and relaxation – perfect for a mid-week break.

Day 6 - Thursday

Sibenik, Skradin, and Sladić Winery

We start early and sail back toward Sibenik, stopping in Skradin, a picturesque town nestled near the Krka National Park. In the hills of Plastovo, we’ll visit Ante Sladić Winery, a historic family-run estate known for producing exceptional wines from indigenous Dalmatian grape varieties.

This unique microclimate, influenced by the Krka River and the Adriatic Sea, gives the wines their distinctive character. After a guided tasting, we’ll enjoy a traditional Dalmatian dinner, featuring local dishes from Skradin – a perfect way to savour the region’s culinary roots.

Day 7 - Friday

A Day to Unwind in Sibenik

On our final full day, we’ll enjoy a leisurely lunch and swim at anchor, taking in the tranquillity of the Adriatic one last time. The evening is free for you to explore Sibenik. Visit its stunning fortresses, wander through its charming old town, or indulge in a final Croatian feast at one of the waterfront restaurants.

Day 8 - Saturday

Farewell to Croatia

It’s time to bid farewell. After breakfast, we’ll disembark in the morning, leaving you with unforgettable memories of Croatia’s dazzling coastline, exquisite wines, and rich culture. We hope to see you again soon!

This journey is not just a holiday – it’s a full sensory experience of Croatia’s vibrant culture, delectable wines, and unspoiled natural beauty. Get ready for an adventure you’ll cherish forever.
