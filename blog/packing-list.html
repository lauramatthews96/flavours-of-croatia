The Ultimate Packing List

December 6, 2024

Packing for a sailing holiday is an art. You want to be prepared without bringing so much that you’re tripping over luggage in tight quarters. Less is more: you’ll live in a swimsuit and something breezy. Choose a soft duffel over a hard suitcase.

Bring two or three swimsuits, lightweight linen, a light jacket for evenings, non-slip deck shoes, reef-safe sunscreen, polarised sunglasses and a wide-brimmed hat. Leave the heels, hair tools and extra outfits at home.
