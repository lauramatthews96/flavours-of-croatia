The Best Time of Year to Sail

November 1, 2024

July and August bring heat, calm seas and festival energy. May–June and September–October are the sweet spot: still warm, quieter, better value, and stronger sailing winds. November to April is serene but cooler, with limited marina services — our bases close from early November to mid-April.
